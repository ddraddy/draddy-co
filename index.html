About Draddy

DANIEL
Draddy

For two decades, Daniel Draddy has been the name that ad agencies, production houses, and Fortune 500 brands reach for when the stakes are high and the vision is ambitious. Known simply as Draddy, he brings a rare combination to every project: the technical precision of a seasoned director of photography and the business fluency of an executive producer.

From orchestrating nationwide commercial shoots for Delta Airlines and Coca-Cola to directing intimate documentary work for veterans returning home, Draddy approaches every frame with the same commitment — tell the truth beautifully.

Based in Atlanta and built for anywhere, Draddy operates as an independent creative executive. His network of trusted collaborators spans every discipline — lighting, audio, talent, post — assembled specifically for each project, every time.
