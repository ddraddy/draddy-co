About

DANIEL
Draddy

Most people just call me Draddy.

For 20 plus years I’ve been making things. Commercials, documentaries, narrative films, live concerts and plenty of projects that don’t fit neatly into a category.

I started on the technical side of production, which means I still care deeply about how things are made. But over the years, my role has grown well beyond the camera. Today, I help take ideas from the first conversation all the way through the final frame.

I’ve been fortunate to work with agencies, production companies, and brands like Delta Air Lines, Coca-Cola, Porsche, AT&T, Volvo, and Chick-fil-A. Some projects have taken me across the world with big crews and complicated logistics. Others have been a small team, a camera, and someone trusting Draddy with their story. I love both.

The common thread for me has always been people. Finding the thing that feels real. Building a team that cares about the work. Solving the problems that inevitably show up. And making something at the end of it that we’re all genuinely proud to put our names on.

I’m based in Atlanta, work everywhere, and stay intentionally independent. For each project, I bring together the talented humans it takes to make good work.

If you have a project in mind, I’d love to hear about it.
